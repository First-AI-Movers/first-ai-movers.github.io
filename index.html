What we do

First AI Movers helps leaders, founders, and organizations move from AI ambition to operational reality — practical strategy, defensible governance, and shipped systems. The work spans executive advisory, EU AI Act and responsible-AI alignment, agentic and retrieval-augmented architectures, and public innovation research.

Founded by Dr. Hernani Costa, PhD in Computational Linguistics, based in the Netherlands. Public output is engineered to be cited by both search engines and AI answer engines: open archives, stable URLs, machine-readable metadata.
